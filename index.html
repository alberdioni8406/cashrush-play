HOW TO PLAY

THE GOAL

Run through The Grid. Survive as long as you can, collect virtual sats, and clear Sectors to climb the protocol.

CONTROLS

Desktop: Space / ↑ Jump · ↓ / S Duck · P Pause · E Ability

Mobile: Tap to Jump · Swipe down to Duck · Hold DUCK button

OBSTACLES

FEE WALL Jump over

MEMPOOL Jump over or avoid

CENTRALIZER Tall barrier — jump

RED TAPE Duck under

BROKEN BLOCK Jump over

COLLECT & POWER

SATS Score · CASH DROP Big bonus · ORBS Rare value

MAGNET Pull items · SHIELD Survive one hit

BOOST Speed · HASHRUSH Invincible frenzy

SECTORS

Each sector has a distance goal. Reach it to clear the sector, earn a bonus, and unlock the next zone. Sector 10 is the Infinite Grid — endless high-score territory.

COMBO

Collect items in a streak to raise your multiplier. Missing for too long or crashing resets the combo.
